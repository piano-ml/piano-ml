Connect an AI coach

Use your own AI assistant as your piano coach

Your own AI assistant can become your piano coach. Connected to PianoML, it reads your practice history, suggests pieces and drives the player. What it reads goes to the AI provider of that assistant, not to PianoML.

3. Play

Turn on Remote coach in a PianoML tab: your assistant can then open scores and exercises, set up the practice, and start or stop the player there. The switch stays at the bottom right of every page; turn it off at any time.

Your browser asks for one click before the first sound: press Start playback when it shows up.
